# Synonyms

Synonyms expand the search query to include words that shoppers might use that differ from those in your catalog. You do not want to lose a sale because someone is looking for a "sofa", while your product is listed as a "couch". Capture a broad range of search terms by entering all the possible words that customers might use to find your products.

The system enables [Semantic search](/help/optimizer/setup/semantic-search.md) by default and matches similar terms and natural language by understanding meaning rather than exact text. Semantic search can reduce the need for some general synonyms. However, you may still want synonyms for brand-specific or technical terms. You can use synonyms and semantic search together.
